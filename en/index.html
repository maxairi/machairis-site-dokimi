FAQ

What our customers usually ask.

What services do you offer?

Pallet storage, picking and packing, cross-docking, container unloading and palletising, plus domestic and international transport and distribution across Attica.

Can I use only storage or only transport?

Yes. You can choose either service on its own, or both together and have one contact for the whole journey.

Where is the warehouse?

At Thesi Skarpa in Aspropyrgos, in the Thriasio area, with quick access to the Attiki Odos motorway and the PATHE national road.

What do you need to send me a quote?

The type of goods, the volume (for example, pallets), how long you need storage and, for transport, the route and how often it runs.

Do you work with forwarders from abroad?

Yes. We receive loads at our Aspropyrgos warehouse and handle cross-docking and distribution within Greece.

Do you have your own trucks?

Yes. We run our own fleet of 5 trucks for domestic transport and distribution.
